| **Terminal workflow** | CLI workflows — explicit verification, scripting, and agent loops |
| **Enterprise/compliance** | managed IDE or extension workflows — filter by policy and deployment needs |

## What Changed in 2025-2026

- Syntax is less of a bottleneck now. Review, verification, and context discipline matter more.
- Bigger context windows did not make sloppy context safe. They mostly made it easier to hide bad habits.
- Teams that get reliable results usually start with a spec, a narrow task, and a way to check the output.
- Subagents are now normal, but mostly as a way to keep research noise out of the main execution loop.

## The Golden Rule

> **Start with work you could solve on your own.** That is how you learn when the model is helping and when it is just sounding confident.
